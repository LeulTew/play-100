## Music and effects

**Play 100 original instrumental**: a new procedural composition authored for
this film, synthesized locally from oscillators and seeded noise. No commercial
recording, borrowed melody, external sample, model or paid service is used.
The reproducible generator and event schedule ship with the composition.
This is an original production asset, not a claim to license any third-party
music. The final video and original bed may be used together for this launch.

**UI click**: Kenney UI Audio, `ui/click2.ogg`, supplied by the installed brag
skill. Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The same restrained click is used at the two Pin actions and Compare action.

The bundled ende.app music is **not used or distributed**. Its bundled README
does not establish redistribution rights; an original local bed was explicitly
approved instead. No license assertion about those unused tracks is made.
