# Play 100 - The original 100: credits and source authority

**Curated by Leul Tewodros Agonafer.**
Website: https://play-100-collection.vercel.app
Public source: https://github.com/LeulTew/play-100
Source release: `046eb1d77a7f6e5d47122a96fda6a20615295b71`.

This is an authorized, source-derived product film about the original
collection, not an official best-games list, current critic survey or claim
that every title was played. The interface is recreated at video scale.
The displayed ratings are real source values, not synthetic opinions.

## Data, ordering and ratings

`public\data\collection.json` supplies exact ranks, titles, years, tiers,
original rating display/raw values/cells and source notes. The film selects
#01 Red Dead Redemption 2, #07 Grand Theft Auto IV and #51 DOOM Eternal.
Original displays are **10.0**, **9.8** and **8.5**, respectively.
No value is reconstructed from rankIndex, position or critic average.

Leul's ratings are identified separately from Metacritic /100 and IGN /10.
The source's unavailable GTA IV IGN value remains a dash, not zero.
The selected records have null source notes. All 12 annotated source records
are preserved in the provenance receipt, not silently normalized or rewritten.
Decorative workbook-sheet rows in CollectionPage.tsx were not a data authority.

The complete selected records, original notes, canonical JSON hash, cover
hashes/dimensions and both workbook hashes are in
`evidence\selected-collection.json`. Both original and enhanced Excel files
remain unchanged. Publication of this video does not export private progress.

## Sound

The 22-second **Play 100 original instrumental** is the sample-free local
procedural composition made for the first launch film, reused with permission
for this complementary film. It uses oscillators and seeded noise, not a
commercial recording, borrowed melody, external sample or paid model.
The generator, full authored event schedule and real 30fps RMS/band analysis
are included in `composition\scripts` and `composition\assets\music`.

Three quiet `click2.ogg` cues come from **Kenney UI Audio**:
https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The CC0 notice accompanies the local file.

There is no voice or narration. No ende.app track is used or distributed;
the bundled music README did not establish redistribution rights and the
approved original bed avoids that uncertainty.
