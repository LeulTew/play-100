Play 100 customizes the body/accent palette, limits the shape/expression
variants, uses restrained markings/blush and disables all animation.
Only Critters is used. Other DiceBear styles may have different licenses.
The original source/creator/license metadata is retained in generated SVGs.
See third-party/dicebear/README.md in the source for the versioned recipe.
